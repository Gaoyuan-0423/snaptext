# 简阅隐私政策 / JianYue Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026-10-22。适用版本：JianYue 0.1.1（3）的 macOS App Store 构建。
开发者：Yuan Gao。联系邮箱：[gaoyuan1799@163.com](mailto:gaoyuan1799@163.com)。

## 本地阅读与存储
简阅处理你主动选择的 TXT 与 EPUB 文件，将图书副本、阅读进度、书签、笔记和偏好设置保存在本机应用数据目录中。应用不要求账号，也不提供云同步。

当前 App Store 构建未授予网络访问权限。EPUB 阅读器不加载远程资源、不执行脚本。图书和阅读记录不由应用发送给开发者。

## 广告与统计
应用没有集成广告、使用行为统计或开发者的崩溃上报服务，也不通过这些服务收集阅读信息。Apple 自身的 App Store、TestFlight 和系统诊断服务受 Apple 的隐私政策及你的系统设置约束。

## 权限与管理
导入文件通过系统文件选择器获得所选文件的读取权限。你可以在书库中删除不再需要的图书。卸载应用本体不一定删除应用数据；需要完整清理或迁移帮助时，请联系支持，并先备份希望保留的内容。

## 主动联系支持
通过邮件联系支持时，邮件服务和收件方会收到你主动发送的邮箱地址、正文及附件。请勿发送不必要的个人信息、私人笔记或完整图书。关于这些邮件的访问、更正或删除请求，可以联系上述邮箱。

## 政策更新
如后续版本的数据处理方式发生变化，本页将同步更新，并标明日期。

## English

Updated: October 22, 2026. Applies to the macOS App Store build JianYue 0.1.1 (3).
Developer: Yuan Gao. Contact: **gaoyuan1799@163.com**.

JianYue processes TXT and EPUB files you choose and stores book copies, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and preferences locally on your Mac. No account or cloud synchronization is provided. This App Store build has no network entitlement. Its EPUB renderer does not load remote resources or execute scripts. The app does not send books or reading records to the developer.

The app includes no advertising, usage analytics or developer-operated crash reporting. Apple's App Store, TestFlight and system diagnostics are governed by Apple's policies and your settings.

The system file picker grants read access to files you select. You may remove books from the library. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove its data. Back up data you want to keep and contact support for cleanup or migration help.

If you email support, your email provider and the recipient receive the address, message and attachments you voluntarily send. Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information, private notes or complete books. Contact the address above with questions or requests relating to support correspondence.

This page will be updated if data practices change in later versions.
